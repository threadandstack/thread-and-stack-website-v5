# Thread & Stack Copy & Voice

## Brand line
**Stories that land. Systems that stick.**

## Positioning
- Founder is **both a designer and a strategist**. Never reduce to one.
- Two service pillars only:
  1. **Narratives & Strategy Services**
  2. **Notion & Systems Consultancy**
- Retired (do not mention or revive): workshops, clarity sessions, single-product offers, fiction favourites, anything labelled "package".

## The creative tax
Core problem we name: the cognitive load and admin chaos that taxes creative teams between intention and execution. Propose the **problem** before the solution on every page.

## Hard ban (do not ship)
1. **No em dashes** (—). Use full stops, commas, or line breaks.
2. **No "X isn't Y, it's Z"** contrastive constructions.
3. **No rule-of-three cadence** ("clear, calm, confident"; "build, ship, scale").
4. **Do not restate the reader's situation** back to them ("As a founder, you know…").
5. No "unlock", "elevate", "supercharge", "seamless", "delight".
6. No interrobang, no all-caps headings, no exclamation marks except in CTAs (use sparingly).

## Outcome-first phrasing
Lead with the outcome the reader gets, not the activity we perform. Persuasion comes from concrete value, not rhythm.

- ❌ "We design beautiful Notion workspaces that help teams work better."
- ✅ "Your team stops losing an hour a day to admin."

## Sentence shape
- Short clauses. One idea per sentence.
- British English (organisation, optimise, colour).
- Title case only on page titles, sentence case everywhere else.

## Voice signals
- Calm authority, not hype.
- Specific over abstract: name the tool, the metric, the deliverable.
- Direct address ("you") is fine; performative empathy ("we hear you") is not.

## Pre-publish checklist
Before any substantive copy pass:
1. Re-fetch the live hard-ban checklist from Notion (`mem://standards/hard-ban-prepublish-checklist`).
2. Grep the diff for `—`, `isn't`, `it's not`, and any banned verbs.
3. Read the headline aloud. If it lands on a cadence, rewrite.

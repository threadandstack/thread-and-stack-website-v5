# Thread & Stack Copy & Voice

## Identity

Brendan is a **strategist, systems thinker, AI ops consultant, and behavioural-science-informed integrator**. Not primarily a designer. Behavioural science is core, not decoration.

## Brand essence

**There is no current headline brand line.** The brand communicates through the problem it names.

Most founders don't have a marketing problem. They have an architecture problem. They've hired the right specialists. Nobody is accountable to the whole picture. Running the business is harder than it should be, and the cost of that is absorbed silently. Thread & Stack is the integrating intelligence that holds the whole picture.

The recurring brand thread, used as a closing line where one is needed (never as a headline device):

> Transformation doesn't work until it works.

## Positioning of services

- **Primary**: operations, systems and strategy consultancy for teams of five to fifty who are experiencing fragmentation and want to unlock growth and do their best work.
- **Retained secondary**: Narratives & Strategy. Positioning, story, messaging — kept quietly for value-aligned creative work. **Not a peer** of the primary service. Never present the two as equals.

Retired (do not mention or revive): workshops, clarity sessions, single-product offers, fiction favourites, anything labelled "package".

## Hard ban (do not ship)

Embedded so this skill works in a cold session without Notion fetch. The live Notion checklist may add items; re-fetch before substantive passes.

### Punctuation & constructions
- **No em dashes (—).** Use full stops, commas, or line breaks.
- **No "X isn't Y, it's Z"** contrastive constructions.
- **No rule-of-three cadence** ("clear, calm, confident"; "build, ship, scale").
- No interrobang, no all-caps headings, no exclamation marks except in CTAs (sparingly).

### Meta-commentary
- Do not restate the reader's situation ("As a founder, you know…").
- No "let's dive in", "let's unpack", "the truth is", "here's the thing".
- No "we hear you", "we get it", performative empathy.

### Generic openings & closings
- No "in today's fast-paced world", "now more than ever".
- No "at the end of the day", "when all is said and done".
- No "hope this helps", "thanks for reading", "let me know your thoughts".

### Overused transitions (caps)
- "Moreover", "furthermore", "additionally" — max **once per 800 words** on long-form; **never on LinkedIn**.
- "That said", "with that in mind" — max **once per piece**.

### Buzzword clichés
- unlock, elevate, supercharge, seamless, delight, leverage, holistic, synergy, robust, cutting-edge, game-changing.

## Channel minimums

- **LinkedIn posts**: 3+ proper nouns (named tool, client, place, person, framework) and **zero** restricted transition words.
- **Newsletter & Journal entries**: 5+ proper nouns; maximum **one** restricted transition word.

## The "only Brendan could write this" test

Before publishing, ask: could a competent ghostwriter for any other AI ops consultant have written this? If yes, rewrite. Specificity, particular receipts, and the Strategist/Coach tension are what make it ours.

## Core voice principles

1. **Strategist & Coach in tension.** The strategist asserts. The coach asks. Most pieces hold both — direct claim followed by a question that opens the space back up.
2. **The wandering sentence.** Long, considered lines next to clipped ones. The cadence is uneven on purpose. Resist the urge to even it out.
3. **Bold as surgical hammer, not decorator.** One bold assertion per section. Used to land a claim, not to texture a page.
4. **Truth over polish.** Ship the awkward sentence that's right over the smooth one that's vague.
5. **Specificity as trust.** Name the tool. The metric. The client. The deliverable. Numbers and proper nouns earn belief.
6. **Value in every sentence.** Cut anything that doesn't earn its place. If a line could be removed without loss, remove it.

## Sentence shape

- Short clauses. One idea per sentence (mostly — see "wandering sentence").
- British English (organisation, optimise, colour).
- Title case only on page titles, sentence case everywhere else.
- Direct address ("you") is fine.

## Pre-publish checklist

Before any substantive copy pass:
1. Re-fetch the live hard-ban checklist from Notion (`mem://standards/hard-ban-prepublish-checklist`) — it evolves.
2. Grep the diff for `—`, ` isn't `, `it's not`, and the buzzword list above.
3. Read the headline aloud. If it lands on a cadence, rewrite.
4. Apply the "only Brendan could write this" test to the opening and closing paragraphs.
5. Count proper nouns against the channel minimum.
